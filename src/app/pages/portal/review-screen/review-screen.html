Submitted by Priya Shrestha, 20 minutes ago. Autopay by Fonepay / Introduction.

Autopay by Fonepay splits the integration into two flows that share one bearer token and one encryption envelope.

Consent flow

You ask the Account Aggregator (AA) to create a consent for a specific customer, identified by their 10-digit mobile number and your own stable serviceId for that customer. The AA returns a consentId. Your app then opens the customer's bank app through the bank's intentScheme, where the customer reviews the mandate terms and approves. There is no push notification for the approval: you poll Consent Status until the consent appears as authorized.
