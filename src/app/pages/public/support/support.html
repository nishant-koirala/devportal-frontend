How do I get production credentials?

Once you have tested your integration in the sandbox environment, you can submit your application for review from the Dashboard. Once approved, production credentials will be issued.

Is there a cost to use the sandbox?

No, the sandbox environment is completely free to use for all registered developers.

What programming languages are supported?

Our APIs are RESTful and can be consumed by any language that supports HTTP requests. We also provide SDKs for Java, Node.js, and Python.
